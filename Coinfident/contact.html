About Coinfident

Coinfident is a student project from Batangas State University - The NEU Alangilan. Its goal is to help college students build responsible money habits through simple budgeting methods and easy-to-understand financial information.

On the Main page you can:

  • Learn how the 50/30/20 rule splits an allowance into needs, wants, and savings.
  • Enter your own numbers and get a Good, Medium, or Poor rating.
  • Open recommended articles and videos on budgeting and saving.
